Madhubani swatch book

The motif library, drawn as real SVG — bezier paths, not SDF primitives. Every motif paints into named roles (ink / fill A / fill B / bead / ground), never hardcoded colours, so the same drawing serves all eight palettes. The shader will only ever place and colour these.

Role contract

These nine classes are the whole vocabulary a motif may use. They are also exactly the channels the baked atlas will store, so the bake is a straight read of the same classes — there is no second contract to keep in sync. Flip on role mask to see the raw channels.
