4. Risks Seen in the Code Concepts, Measurements & Architecture

These are the concrete technical risks already visible from your measurements and architecture (to be sharpened with exact line references once the files are read):

Confounded Error in AUDIT.md (6 of 7 Fore/Hind Load Splits Wrong + 60.0% CoM Height):

Uniform-density voxel integration treats an air-filled chest/ribcage as solid muscle (~1,000 kg/m³) and thick furry tails/heads as heavy meat. Because quadruped lungs sit anterior to the CoM, uniform density artificially shifts mass forward — which alone can corrupt fore/hind load splits!

Risk: If we feed uncalibrated mass splits straight into Jolt, real physics will faithfully simulate front-heavy noses. Conversely, if the upstream SDFs truly have templated anatomy (60.0% CoM height, 8.6% spread across 13 species), changing density won't fix it. Task 2.1 must disambiguate ruler error from sculpt error before M3.

Allometric Leverage Artifact (0.75 With Elephant vs. 0.65 Without):

A single 4,000 kg animal sitting 2–3 orders of magnitude beyond the 24 upstream species has extreme statistical leverage on a log-log slope. A 0.10 shift from one body is a warning sign that the upstream 24 bodies follow a different scaling law (0.65) than the authored elephant, and that the 0.75 figure could be an illusion of leverage rather than a property of the dataset.

The 29× Fox FulliK Residual (0.90 mm Front-Left vs. 2.59 cm Hind-Left) + Partial Chain Wiring:

The prompt lists chains for "spine, front-left, hind-left, ear and jaw" — missing the right-side limbs and tail.

A 2.59 cm residual while the sibling front leg hits 0.90 mm strongly points to one of four bugs in fox-fullik.ts: (a) reverse-bend hock lock under generic ball-joint cone limits, (b) end-effector placed at ankle/metatarsal instead of paw pad (~2.5 cm off the ground), (c) stale pelvis socket if spine and hind-leg solve in the wrong order, or (d) pelvis height exceeding hind-leg reach. This must be fixed in M2, not papered over by physics springs in M3.

Madhubani Mip Washing & Extreme-Bend Distortion:

Hand-built mips on thin black Mithila linework (especially dense katchni hatching and godna patterns) naturally average black ink + bright pigment into dull grey at distance — which is almost certainly a primary contributor to why "the toon/banded shading still does not read at distance."

Secondary risk once physics lands: real drops, steep slopes, and dynamic elephant trunk curling bend joints far harder than hand-authored walk cycles, stressing dual-quaternion skinning and compressing (around, along) metres on inner creases.

JoltPhysics.js WASM Memory Leaks & Inertia Tensor Crashes:

Emscripten bindings (JoltPhysics.js) do not garbage-collect C++ objects (new Jolt.Vec3(), RVec3, Quat, BodyCreationSettings). Any allocation inside the 60 Hz step loop silently leaks WASM memory until the tab crashes.

Jolt strictly validates rigid-body mass properties; non-positive-definite or degenerate inertia tensors from thin SDF segments (e.g., thin ear/tail voxels) will throw or destabilize constraints. Every segment exported by mass.ts must pass a positive-definite + minimum-mass/radius floor check.

Premature handwired Coupling Risk:

handwired compiles JS logic into differentiable ReGLU/matmul networks[1], which introduces approximation bounds (tracked in your PORT-REPORT.md). Keeping handwired strictly offline inside madhuban-morphology/ — and never inside the live browser Three.js/Jolt runtime — is critical for maintaining 60 fps and exact determinism.

1. Architecture Read & Call on IK-versus-Dynamics
Definitive Call: Split-Authority Dynamics (Reject Both Naive Extremes)

The boundary between JoltPhysics and FulliK should be neither a full dynamic ragdoll with post-physics IK clamping nor pure kinematic animation with passive constraints. Divide every physics-enabled animal into three authorities:

Dynamic Core in Jolt (Mass, Momentum & Balance Authority):

Pelvis + thorax (1 or 2 dynamic rigid bodies linked by a spring-damped SwingTwistConstraint) driven by real mass, local CoM, and positive-definite 3×3 inertia tensors from madhuban-morphology/mass.ts.

Why This Beats Both Alternatives

Against Active Ragdoll + Post-IK: Jolt’s DriveToPoseUsingMotors acts in local joint space and provides no world-space root balance torque. Standing a 35-joint dynamic animal across 6 body plans and a ~1,000× mass span (fox to elephant) requires per-body-plan closed-loop balance controllers and 120–240 Hz substeps, and post-physics FulliK foot clamping fights Jolt’s contact solver—causing silhouette jitter that directly violates Art first. Furthermore, PLAN-form-follows-function.md confirms the fore/hind load split is currently wrong in 6 of 7 force-plate-referenced animals[3]; a free active ragdoll on uncalibrated mass distribution will simply pitch onto its nose or sit back on its haunches.

Against Passive Constraints Only: Kinematic gaits with height/limit clamping fake inertia and scale; an elephant and a fox land and turn identically unless hand-authored, and the engine gains no real force/dynamics layer to validate derived morphologies.
